# Micro-Commitment Ladder for Website Pitching

Never ask a cold prospect for a 30-minute meeting or a buying decision upfront. High-conversion sales (90%+ close on engaged leads) is achieved by stacking tiny, low-friction "yeses".

## The 5 Rungs of the Ladder:

1. **Rung 1: Agreement to Receive (Zero Risk)**
   - *"Would it be okay if I share a 30-second preview link with you here?"*
   - Outcome: Prospect agrees to receive value. No sales pressure.

2. **Rung 2: Feedback Agreement (Opinion Asking)**
   - *"Did the demo open smoothly on your phone? What do you think of the Mercedes service booking button?"*
   - Outcome: Prospect evaluates your work on their own terms.

3. **Rung 3: Problem Confirmation (Admitting Pain)**
   - *"Does your current website generate at least 5 WhatsApp booking inquiries a week, or is it mostly dead?"*
   - Outcome: Prospect acknowledges the status quo is leaking revenue.

4. **Rung 4: Solution Alignment (Assumptive Choice)**
   - *"Would you prefer incoming customer bookings routed directly to your personal WhatsApp or your service manager's desk?"*
   - Outcome: Psychological ownership of the system.

5. **Rung 5: The Launch Decision (Low-Risk Commitment)**
   - *"We can have this live on your actual domain in 48 hours without any downtime to your emails. Should we proceed with the 50% launch deposit?"*
   - Outcome: Signed deal.
