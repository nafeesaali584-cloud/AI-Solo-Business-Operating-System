# Auto Workshop Objection Handling Playbook

When pitching website redesigns to UAE garage owners, objections are not rejections. They are buying signals or defense mechanisms from busy owners. Use this playbook to guide your responses.

## Core Formula: The ACRC Method
1. **Acknowledge**: Validate their position ("Totally understand", "Makes complete sense").
2. **Clarify**: Strip away surface excuses to reveal the real issue.
3. **Respond**: Reframe using ROI, proof, or reverse psychology.
4. **Confirm**: Close with a low-friction question or pause.

---

## 1. "How much does it cost?" (Early in conversation)
*Psychology*: Coach Animesh Price Anchoring. Never give a raw low number without anchoring first.
*WhatsApp Script*:
> "Fair question [Name]! A full agency in Dubai typically charges AED 12,000 to AED 18,000 for this caliber of custom build.
> 
> Because we already built your functional demo upfront, we can deploy the complete modern system—including direct WhatsApp booking and Google speed optimization—for just **AED 3,800 one-time**.
> 
> That's usually paid off by just 1 or 2 major European brake or transmission service jobs.
> 
> Did you get a chance to open the preview link above?"

---

## 2. "I already have a website / I already have a web guy"
*Psychology*: Sit on the same side of the table (Hormozi). Don't insult their existing guy. Show the specific mobile leak.
*WhatsApp Script*:
> "Completely respect that [Name], and having an existing setup is great. 
> 
> The only reason I created this demo for you is that when customers search your garage on mobile in [Location], the booking buttons take 6+ seconds to load, which causes 60% of smartphone users to tap back to Google and call another garage.
> 
> You don't even have to hire us—you can literally take our demo and give the layout ideas to your web guy for free so your garage stops losing those calls.
> 
> Mind if I send you the 30-sec link just for your feedback?"

---

## 3. "We get all our business through word-of-mouth, we don't need a website"
*Psychology*: Validate word-of-mouth as #1, but reframe the website as the "verification trust filter".
*WhatsApp Script*:
> "100%, [Name]! For top garages in the UAE, word-of-mouth will always be #1. 
> 
> Here's what we see happening today: when a customer recommends [Garage Name] to a friend with an Audi or Mercedes, that friend immediately Googles your name before driving to Al Quoz.
> 
> If your site looks outdated or broken on their phone, they hesitate and question if you handle high-end cars.
> 
> Our redesign is built specifically to protect your reputation and convert those word-of-mouth referrals instantly.
> 
> Worth a quick 30-second look at the demo?"

---

## 4. "Send me all the details on WhatsApp / Email"
*Psychology*: Prevent the endless brush-off trap. Give a concise summary + 1 micro-commitment.
*WhatsApp Script*:
> "Done! Here are the 3 quick details:
> 
> 1. **Live Preview**: {{demo_link}}
> 2. **What's Solved**: 1-click WhatsApp booking, 1.2s mobile loading, showcase of luxury car certifications & Google Reviews.
> 3. **Turnaround**: 48 hours to launch on your domain with zero downtime.
> 
> Have a look on your phone when you have 60 seconds and let me know if you like the layout or if you'd tweak anything."

---

## 5. "It's too expensive / We don't have budget right now"
*Psychology*: Rajiv Talreja Perceived Value + Hormozi Value Equation (Cost of Inaction).
*WhatsApp Script*:
> "Totally understand cash flow is priority, [Name].
> 
> Just to see if it makes sense: what is an average ticket for a major service or brake overhaul at your workshop? (Usually AED 1,500 – AED 3,500).
> 
> If having this modern site brings in just **1 additional client next month** who otherwise would have booked with a competitor, the entire system is 100% free profit after that.
> 
> We can also split it into 2 milestones (50% now, 50% only after you are 100% happy and live). Fair enough?"

---

## 6. Prospect Goes Completely Silent (Ghosted after looking at demo)
*Psychology*: The Negative Reverse / Coach Animesh Frame Control.
*WhatsApp Script*:
> "Hey [Name], following up quickly—I'm guessing a website upgrade isn't on your radar right now, or you're completely booked at the garage?
> 
> If so, no worries at all! Just let me know if I should take down your demo preview link so we can allocate that server space to another workshop."
*(Triggers the fear of loss / polite correction: "No no, I was just busy, let me check today!")*
