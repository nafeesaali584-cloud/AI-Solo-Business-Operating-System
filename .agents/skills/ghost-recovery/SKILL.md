# Ghost Recovery Framework (UAE B2B)

When a garage owner stops replying after seeing your demo or discussing price, they are rarely rejecting you. 85% of the time, an emergency at the workshop occurred (customs parts delay, customer dispute, mechanic shortage, rush of cars).

## Ghost Recovery Rules:
1. **Never send passive-aggressive bumps**: Never write "Bumping this to the top of your inbox" or "Did you see my message?". They add zero value and project weakness.
2. **Always introduce a new angle or takeaway**: Every re-engagement message must stand on its own feet.
3. **Use the "Relief Valve"**: Give them an easy way out. Paradoxically, letting them off the hook makes them respond.

### Play 1: The Competitor Shift (Day 4 after silence)
> "Hey [Name], saw you were busy. Quick question—we had another European specialist garage in [Location] reach out asking about a similar direct-booking layout. 
> 
> Before we commit our development sprint to them, I wanted to check if you still wanted to use the custom demo we made for [Garage Name]?"

### Play 2: The Screenshot Teaser (Day 7 after silence)
*(Send 1 clean mobile screenshot of their service booking page)*
> "[Name], here is a 1-second preview of how your customer reviews and WhatsApp button look on iPhone. 
> 
> Did you get a chance to test the preview link?"

### Play 3: The 9-Word Email / WhatsApp (Day 11 after silence)
> "Hey [Name], are you still looking to upgrade [Garage Name]'s website this season?"

### Play 4: The Clean Breakup (Day 16 after silence)
> "Hey [Name], reaching out one last time. Since I haven't heard back, I assume this isn't a priority right now, which is completely fine!
> 
> I'm going to take down the temporary demo server for [Garage Name] on Friday so our team can archive it.
> 
> Wish you and the garage continued success!"
